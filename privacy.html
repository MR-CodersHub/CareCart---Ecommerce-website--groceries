1. Our Privacy Pledge & Zero Data Selling

At CareCart, we believe senior citizens and their caregivers deserve the highest tier of data confidentiality and respect.

Our Zero Data-Selling Promise: CareCart does not sell, rent, or trade senior or caregiver personal information, dietary habits, or health preferences to advertisers, insurance brokers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

All data collected is used solely to provide accurate grocery fulfillments, ensure food safety compliance, and facilitate family caregiver coordination.

2. Information We Collect & Why

We collect only the minimum necessary information to ensure timely and safe grocery services:

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email, and password.
  • Delivery Addresses: Street address, landmarks, doorstep instructions (e.g., "Ring bell twice, elevator code").
  • Dietary & Health Restrictions: Optional tags provided by seniors or caregivers (e.g., Diabetic-Friendly, Low Sodium, Peanut Allergy, Lactose Intolerant).
  • Caregiver Relationship Links: Authorizations linking family caregivers to a senior's delivery schedule.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (UPI, Cards, NetBanking). CareCart never stores raw credit card CVVs.

3. How We Protect Health & Dietary Data

We treat dietary preferences, allergy tags, and meal planning notes as sensitive personal data:

Automated Safety Filtering: When you record an allergy (such as Gluten or Shellfish), our catalog search engine automatically flags or blocks items containing those allergens from being accidentally added to your cart.

Access Restrictions: Delivery drivers and third-party logistics agents only see product names and delivery instructions. They are never granted access to senior medical histories, caregiver notes, or dietary restriction profiles.

4. Caregiver Sharing & Visibility Controls

CareCart's unique caregiver feature allows family members to stay informed while maintaining senior independence:

  • Authorized Visibility: Linked caregivers can view order history, delivery tracking, and update dietary guidelines.
  • Senior Authorization: Caregiver links are only activated with verified senior consent (via SMS OTP or phone verification).
  • Instant Revocation: Seniors can remove or modify caregiver access at any time directly through their account settings or via our phone hotline.

5. Your Rights, Data Deletion & Contact DPO

You have full control over your information at all times:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request an export of your order history and profile information in digital format.
  • Right to Correction: Update your address, contact numbers, or preferences instantly via our portal.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated records by emailing our Data Protection Officer.
