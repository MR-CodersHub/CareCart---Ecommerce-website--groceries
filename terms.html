Weight Discrepancies & Pricing: For fresh produce priced by weight, slight variations may occur during handpicking. You will only be billed for the exact weight fulfilled.

Substitutions Protocol: If an item is out of stock, our personal shoppers will respect the senior's preference (Best Match, Contact Caregiver First, or Do Not Substitute). Unsafe items that violate a registered senior allergy will never be substituted.

4. Delivery Services & Doorstep Care Protocols

CareCart delivery personnel are background-verified and trained in senior-friendly courtesy standards:

  • Delivery Windows: Orders are delivered within the customer's selected time window. Delivery tracking and driver ETA are communicated via SMS/WhatsApp to both senior and caregiver.
  • Doorstep Assistance: Upon senior request, delivery executives are authorized to place heavy parcels (flour bags, water, oil cans) inside the entryway or kitchen counter.
  • Safe Handover: If a senior does not answer the door, our driver will contact the linked caregiver prior to leaving items or rescheduling, ensuring senior safety.
