Senior-Friendly & Assisted Ordering

Everything you need to know about making grocery shopping easy and accessible.

Section 2

Caregiver Portal & Remote Family Care

How family members can manage grocery needs for seniors from anywhere in the world.

In your Caregiver Dashboard, navigate to "Senior Profile" > "Add Senior Member". Enter your parent's phone number, and a one-time approval SMS will be sent to verify. Once linked, you can manage orders, addresses, and dietary filters together.
Caregivers can specify known allergies (e.g., Peanuts, Gluten) and health conditions (Diabetic, Low Sodium) in the Preferences tab. CareCart automatically flags or blocks items in the catalog that conflict with these restrictions, preventing unsafe purchases.
Yes! You can designate Primary and Secondary caregivers (e.g., son, daughter, or home nurse). Each caregiver receives order updates, delivery confirmations, and can review upcoming scheduled baskets.

Section 3

Delivery, Freshness & Quality Guarantees

Standards governing doorstep assistance, temperature-controlled delivery, and returns.

Yes! All CareCart delivery personnel are background-checked and instructed to provide Doorstep Unpacking Assistance upon request. Drivers will place heavy bags on the kitchen counter or designated entryway table.
We utilize certified insulated cooling totes with food-safe ice gel packs to maintain optimal refrigeration temperatures for milk, curd, paneer, and perishables from our temperature-controlled hub directly to your door.
We provide a 100% No-Questions-Asked 24-Hour Return Guarantee. Simply tap "Report Issue" in your order details or call support. An instant replacement or immediate refund will be issued to your wallet or bank account.
