Our Story

Built Around Real Everyday Needs

Grocery shopping can become difficult when mobility, technology, or daily routines make traditional shopping inconvenient. CareCart was created to provide a simpler alternative.

The platform connects seniors with their family members and caregivers, making it easier to manage grocery lists, dietary preferences, delivery addresses, and recurring orders.
